2. Description of Service

NespPay provides open-source, non-custodial payment routing software. The Service allows businesses to derive unique public cryptocurrency addresses for transactions, track on-chain statuses, and receive payment notifications.

3. Non-Custodial Nature of the Service

  • Zero Custody: NespPay does not hold, manage, store, or transmit your funds or private keys at any point.
